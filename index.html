How the fairness check works

Verified data in, a clear flag out

1. Verified vs. community

Every submission starts as community-reported. It's promoted to verified only when a registered buyer submits it, a second independent entry corroborates it within 72 hours, or an admin confirms it.

2. Median, not mean

The reference price is the median of verified entries from the trailing 30 days, after excluding statistical outliers -- deliberately resistant to a single bad-faith submission.

3. Fair, low, or falling short

Offers within 10% of the reference are Fair; 10-25% below is Below average; more than 25% below is Significantly underpaid -- always shown with a confidence label.
